BOUQUET ONE

Our little story.

From a tiny crush in a tuition class to finally becoming us. ♡

♡

If someone had told me back then that the boy sitting in front of me would someday become my boyfriend...

I probably wouldn't have believed them.
♡
01 · 12TH STANDARD

The boy sitting in front of me.

We first met at tuition in 12th. You were sitting in front of me with your friends...

And somewhere between all those normal tuition classes, I developed a tiny little crush on you.

I used to secretly look at you when you weren't looking. ♡

♡
02 · THE SECRET

“He likes you too.”

Then my classmate told me that you had a crush on me too.

I was shocked... but secretly very, very happy.

The only problem was that I didn't want a relationship then.

So I told my classmate that I didn't like you.

Which was... definitely not completely true. 😂❤️

♡
03 · THE FIRST CONVERSATION

“What is the maths test?”

I still remember the first time you actually talked to me.

You asked me what the maths test was. Such a tiny conversation... but I remember it.

And then you called me. Well... it was a missed call.

And I was ridiculously happy about it. ♡

♡
04 · DIFFERENT COLLEGES

Maybe the story wasn't over.

Then we went to different colleges. I had a public account, and you would like and comment on my posts.

I thought maybe you were just doing it because... well, you never really talked to me.

Then you sent me a request on my private account.

And I accepted. ♡

♡
05 · THE FONT 😂

Apparently, it started with a font.

And what did I message you about?

Your story's font.

You told me which font it was. I said thank you. And that was basically it.

Except... the next day you texted me something and then unsent it.

And eventually, you confessed that you'd had a crush on me back in tuition too.

♡
06 · LET'S SEE WHERE THIS GOES

We decided to take it slow.

We decided to talk, take things slowly, and just see where everything goes.

And honestly... we had our ups and downs.

Sometimes we stopped talking. Then we started again. We argued. We figured things out.

Somehow, we kept finding our way back to each other. ♡

♡
07 · SOMEWHERE ALONG THE WAY

You said “I love you.”

We talked for around two months, and then you confessed that you loved me.

I was always the one saying that love is a very big thing. I didn't want to say it unless I actually meant it.

But somewhere in between all our conversations, arguments, little moments and everything in between...

I realized that I loved you too.

And of course, I confessed it in the middle of an argument. 😂❤️

♥
08 · OCTOBER 1, 2026

Finally... us.

Then you asked me to be your girlfriend.

And I told you that I needed time.

But you never once made me feel like I was making you wait.

You never made me feel guilty. You just quietly kept giving your effort and giving your all.

And I'll always be grateful that you never gave up on us.

01 · 10 · 2026 We became official. ♡

My pretty boyfriend is finally, officially mine. 🥹❤️

And this is still only the beginning...

I can't wait for the chapters we haven't written yet. ♡

Maybe it's when the words suddenly feel a little too personal.

Or maybe it's when you listen to it and imagine a future memory that hasn't even happened yet.

That's what this song feels like to me.

It feels like you. ♡

It makes me think about everything we've already been through...

The tiny crush. The tuition classes. The first conversations. The missed call. Different colleges. The random messages. All our ups and downs. And somehow finding our way back to each other.

And now...

we're actually us.

Maybe that's why this song feels so much more special now.

Because when I listen to it, I don't just hear a song.

I think about you.

I think about the day we'll finally meet.

I think about the first time I'll actually see you standing in front of me.

My Aru,

If you're reading this because you miss me...

I MISS YOU TOO.

More than I probably tell you.

There are so many moments when I randomly wish you were here.

Like when something funny happens and you're the first person I want to tell.

Or when I hear a song and suddenly it reminds me of you.

Or when I'm having one of those days where I just want someone to hold me.

And then I think...

“I wish my bby was here.”

I wish I could just call you, look at you and instead of seeing your face on a screen... actually see you standing right in front of me.

I wish I could hear your voice without headphones.

I wish I could sit next to you without worrying about the distance between us.

I wish I could randomly hold your hand.

And most of all...

I wish I could hug you.

A real hug. A really, really tight hug.

The kind where I don't want to let go immediately.

I already know that when we finally meet, I'll probably be nervous, excited, happy and maybe a little shy all at once.

But the moment I see you, I think all I'll want to do is run towards you and hug you.

And I'm not letting go. 🥹

I want to hold your hand, talk about completely random things, walk with you, go shopping with you and just exist beside you.

I want to kiss every part of your cute face.

I want all those tiny ordinary moments with you.

I LOVE YOU.

And I don't mean it as just a cute little sentence.

I genuinely love the person you are.

I love your smile. I love your eyes. I love your cute and clingy side. I love it when you act like my chotu sa pyaara sa cutu bby.

I love how deeply you care. I love how much effort you make. I love that you're willing to change and put effort into things.

I love how you can keep your ego aside for us.

I love you because you love deeply.

I love you because you are you.

I just love every part of you.

And I especially love that you never made me feel bad for needing time.

You waited. You stayed. You kept giving your effort.

And you never made me feel like I owed you something for it.

I'll always be grateful for that.

We haven't had our first date yet.

We don't have our first picture together yet.

We haven't had our first hug.

We haven't even stood next to each other properly.

But somehow...

you're already such a huge part of my life.

So whenever you miss me, come back to this letter.

Read it again.

And imagine me sitting right next to you, looking at you and saying...

“I love youuuuu so much.” ❤️

I am so proud of you. Proud of everything you do, everything you're working towards, and everything you're becoming.

I want to see you successful. I want to see you happy. And I want to be there beside you while you become everything you want to be.

I believe in you. Always.

Please take care of yourself for me.

Eat properly. Sleep properly. Go to the gym. Enjoy your life. Don't stress too much.

And whenever life feels too much, remember that you have someone who is always cheering for you.

Me. ♡

I don't know exactly what the future is going to look like.

But I know that I want those memories with you.

Our first meeting. Our first hug. Our first picture. Our first date. Our late-night walks. Our bike rides. Our random shopping trips. Our stupid conversations. Our little home.

And one day, I want to look at you and think...

“Finally. You're actually here.”

And then I'm going to hug you so tightly. ❤️

Maybe I'll cry. Maybe I'll laugh. Maybe I'll do both.

But whatever happens...

I'm not letting go.

I LOVE YOUUUUU. ❤️

I MISS YOUUUUU. 🥹

Always yours,
Mannat ♡
